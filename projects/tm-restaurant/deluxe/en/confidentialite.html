Privacy policy

1. Data collected

Through our forms (contact, booking) we only collect: name, email, phone number, date and the content of your message. This data is used solely to process your request.

2. Legal basis and retention

Processing is based on your explicit consent (submitting the form). Messages are kept for a maximum of 12 months, booking requests for 24 months.

3. Sharing

Your data is never sold or passed on to third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Cookies & audience measurement

This site does not use advertising cookies. If audience measurement (Google Analytics) is enabled, it relies on the visitor's prior consent in accordance with the GDPR.

5. Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability. Write to us at reservation@lalchimie-demo.be or by post to L'Alchimie, 210 Avenue Louise, 1050 Brussels.

6. Data controller

L'Alchimie — 210 Avenue Louise, 1050 Brussels — reservation@lalchimie-demo.be.
