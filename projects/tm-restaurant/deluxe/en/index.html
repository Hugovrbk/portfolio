Welcome to L'Alchimie

The chef offers an author's cuisine that blends tradition and modernity, in an elegant and intimate setting. Every plate is designed as an experience in its own right.

Our philosophy

Market-driven cuisine with no compromise on quality, where every ingredient is elevated to reveal the best of Belgian terroir and its partner producers.
